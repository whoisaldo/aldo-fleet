# Optional Canvas LMS planning

Canvas LMS can support planning and organization alongside your existing tools. Depending on the integration available to you, useful options include:

- Reviewing schedules and due dates.
- Summarizing announcements and organizing learning materials.
- Bringing relevant dates into a calendar or task system you already use.
- Getting a concise planning summary from an assistant.

Choose only the capabilities that fit your needs. Live access requires your own authorized account and a supported integration; this bank provides a general planning guide.

If you decide to connect Canvas, start with the options supported by your institution and the [official Canvas developer documentation](https://developerdocs.instructure.com/services/canvas). Keep account credentials, course identifiers, records and personalized schedules private. Verify the intended scope before enabling recurring actions or writing to another tool.

OpenClaw's [visual canvas](openclaw-canvas.md) is available separately for documents, widgets and dashboards.
