# Form and survey review

Read the form the user asks you to review, using their authorized access. Account for required fields, conditional questions, supporting documents and any visible completion requirements.

Keep review drafts offline: entering answers may autosave them. Read later pages through the form's ordinary controls when possible; if they require answers, report which requirements remain unread. Do not insert dummy answers or change validation to obtain access.

Separate requirements from suggestions and personal attestations. Do not invent personal facts. Return a concise checklist with any unresolved fields and unread scope. This skill supports review; saving answers or submitting a form requires the user's request for that action.
